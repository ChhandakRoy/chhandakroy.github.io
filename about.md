I'm Chhandak Roy, a VLSI-focused engineer interested in **RTL design, FPGA architecture and ASIC implementation**.

I like learning by building: turning specifications into hardware, debugging the unexpected behavior, and then understanding what the tools reveal about the implementation.

This site is where I keep the technical notes that come out of that process.

### Areas I work on

- Verilog / RTL design
- Digital interfaces and controllers
- FPGA architecture and acceleration
- ASIC synthesis and timing analysis
- Hardware debugging and design optimization
